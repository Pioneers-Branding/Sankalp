HOW TO ADD YOUR GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE PHOTOS
================================================

The Photos & Reviews page ( /photos ) automatically displays any images
placed in THIS folder under a "Straight from our Google Business Profile"
section. No code changes are needed.

STEPS
-----
1. Open your Google Business Profile:
   https://www.google.com/maps?cid=16523409274206675794
   (or Business Profile Manager -> Photos)

2. Because you OWN the profile, you can download your own photos:
   - Business Profile Manager  ->  Photos  ->  select a photo  ->  Download
   - Or open each photo on Google Maps and "Save image as..."

3. Drop the downloaded .jpg / .png / .webp files into this folder:
   assets/img/google-business/

4. (Recommended) Rename files to descriptive names, e.g.
   reception-desk.jpg, waiting-lounge.jpg, ivf-lab.jpg
   The page turns the file name into a caption automatically
   (reception-desk.jpg  ->  "Reception Desk").

That's it. Refresh /photos and the new photos appear in the gallery.

NOTE: Prefer downloading and re-hosting the photos here (as above) rather
than hot-linking Google's lh3.googleusercontent.com URLs directly -- those
URLs rotate over time and will eventually break on your site.
